# SAMPLE — WAN escalation guide (replace with your own)

Escalate sustained WAN utilization above 85% for more than 30 minutes to the Network Operations on-call engineer.
Before escalating, collect: interface utilization graph for the last 24 hours, error and discard counters,
and the list of scheduled transfers from the service owner.
Carrier tickets are opened only by the on-call engineer after internal checks.
